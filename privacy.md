# IconAnchor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

IconAnchor is developed by Peter Verhoeven.

## Information used by the app

IconAnchor reads desktop item names and paths, icon positions,
and monitor configuration information to remember and restore
desktop icon arrangements. Paths may include your Windows
username or personally identifying file names.

The app observes mouse button actions and pointer positions
to detect deliberate desktop icon moves. It does not record
keystrokes or read the contents of your documents.

## Local storage

Desktop layout information is saved on your computer.
IconAnchor does not send this information to the developer
or other parties.

IconAnchor includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

## Your controls

You can pause icon positioning or exit IconAnchor using its
system tray menu. You can also enable or disable startup
with Windows.

For the current standalone version, you can delete saved layouts
by exiting IconAnchor and deleting the Data folder beside
IconAnchor.exe.

## Microsoft Store and website services

Microsoft processes Store purchases and may collect information
under its own privacy policy.

This privacy-policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages.
GitHub may process website visitor information under its
own privacy statement. This is separate from the IconAnchor app.

## Contact

For privacy questions, visit:
https://github.com/PeterVerhoevenUSA/iconanchor/issues

Do not post private file paths or other sensitive information
in public issues.

## Changes

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change.
